# Preface｜前言

## From Intelligence to Human Life｜從智能到人的生活

### English Source｜American English

OceanAI began with a question about the future of artificial intelligence, but not the question that dominates most discussions of AI.

The question was not simply how intelligent machines might become.

It was what would happen when intelligence crossed the boundaries of an interface and became part of physical space.

What changes when intelligence enters the places where human life unfolds?

What happens when intelligence must do more than answer a question—when it must perceive changing conditions, understand context, coordinate devices, maintain state, operate through time, survive interruption, and act appropriately without requiring people to continuously manage it?

And what happens to human life when this intelligence becomes part of the environment?

These questions became the intellectual origin of OceanAI.

They also became the starting point of this book.

### Wisdom and Intelligence｜智慧與智能

The title *Ocean of Intelligence* begins with an important distinction.

Wisdom and intelligence are related, but they are not the same.

Intelligence perceives, learns, distinguishes, predicts, reasons, decides, coordinates, and increasingly acts.

Wisdom asks a different order of questions.

What is worth knowing?

What should be done?

When should we refrain from acting?

What kind of life should our technologies make possible?

Artificial intelligence dramatically expands the reach of intelligence. But greater intelligence does not automatically produce greater wisdom.

Indeed, the more intelligence humanity brings into the world, the more necessary wisdom becomes.

Intelligence may tell us what can be optimized.

Wisdom asks what is worth optimizing.

Intelligence may discover the fastest route.

Wisdom asks where we should be going.

This book therefore begins with intelligence, but it does not end there.

Its destination is human life.

### From Interface to Physical Space｜從介面進入物理空間

For most of the history of computing, people have approached intelligence through interfaces.

The form of the interface has changed, but the underlying relationship has remained familiar:

**the human initiates the interaction.**

Physical space changes that relationship.

Space exists continuously.

Conditions change whether or not someone is interacting with a system.

Light changes.

Temperature changes.

Air changes.

Energy flows.

Devices change state.

People move through space, carrying intentions that are rarely expressed as explicit commands.

Intelligence in physical space therefore cannot depend entirely on interaction.

It must understand context.

It must operate through time.

It must coordinate.

It must know when to act.

And equally important, it must know when not to act.

This leads to the core question of this book:

> **How can artificial intelligence enter our physical spaces without making us live inside complex AI systems?**

The wording matters.

The goal is not simply to bring AI into more objects.

Nor is it to surround people with more interfaces.

The deeper challenge is to allow intelligence to become part of physical space while preventing the complexity behind that intelligence from becoming a new burden on human life.

**System complexity should be absorbed by the system, not transferred to the human.**

This principle runs throughout the book.

### Space and Environment｜空間與環境

Two words will appear repeatedly in the pages that follow: *Space* and *Environment*.

They are related, but they are not interchangeable.

**Space is the physical setting in which intelligence enters, operates, and coordinates.**

Space is where devices exist, networks connect, systems operate, and relationships acquire physical form.

In this sense, *Space* belongs primarily to the language of architecture, runtime, and the physical world.

**Environment is the total set of conditions that people experience within a space.**

Light, air, temperature, sound, energy, safety, device behavior, and the relationships among people, technology, and their surroundings together form an environment.

In this sense, *Environment* belongs increasingly to the language of experience, human life, and philosophy.

The distinction gives us an important progression:

```text
Physical Space｜物理空間
        ↓
Intelligence Enters｜智能進入
        ↓
Runtime Operates｜運行時運行
        ↓
Conditions Interact｜條件相互作用
        ↓
Environment｜環境
        ↓
Human Experience｜人的體驗
        ↓
Human Life｜人的生活
```

Space is where intelligence enters and operates.

Environment is what intelligence helps shape around human life.

### The Architecture of Intelligence｜智能的架構

Once intelligence enters physical space, intelligence alone is no longer enough.

It needs structure.

A model can generate an answer.

A physical system must maintain relationships.

State must persist. Events must move. Actions must reach the correct destination. Local conditions must be understood. Failures must be contained. Privacy must have boundaries. Essential intelligence must remain available even when parts of a larger system are not.

This is where architecture enters the story.

```text
Artificial Intelligence｜人工智慧
        ↓
Intelligence｜智能
        ↓
Architecture｜架構
        ↓
System｜系統
        ↓
Runtime｜運行時
        ↓
Physical Space｜物理空間
        ↓
Environment｜環境
        ↓
Human Life｜人的生活
```

This sequence is not intended as a rigid technical stack.

It is the intellectual route of the book.

We begin with the nature of intelligence.

We ask how intelligence acquires structure.

We examine what continuity requires.

We follow intelligence into systems and runtime.

We then enter physical space.

And finally, we ask what all of this means for the environment in which people live.

### Continuity｜連續性

Physical life does not operate in isolated sessions.

This simple fact has profound consequences.

If intelligence becomes part of the environment in which people live, continuity becomes a fundamental requirement.

**Physical life does not pause when a remote service becomes unavailable.**

**A space should not lose its basic ability to operate because a network path disappears.**

**Nor should a system treat every moment as if the world had just begun.**

Intelligence must persist through time.

A system must retain enough state to understand change.

It must continue through interruption.

It must adapt without losing coherence.

This is why Runtime becomes important in the OceanAI story.

Runtime is not merely a software mechanism.

In this book, it represents the point at which intelligence acquires continuity.

And continuity carries responsibility.

The more deeply technology enters human life, the more important reliability, resilience, privacy, restraint, and human control become.

This is why the subtitle of this book joins two ideas:

**The Architecture of Intelligence and the Ethics of Continuity.**

Architecture asks how intelligence should be organized.

The ethics of continuity asks what responsibilities arise when that intelligence becomes persistent within human life.

### The Course of This Book｜本書的思想航線

The voyage of *Ocean of Intelligence* therefore follows a deliberate course:

```text
Wisdom｜智慧
        ↓
Artificial Intelligence｜人工智慧
        ↓
Intelligence｜智能
        ↓
Architecture｜架構
        ↓
Continuity｜連續性
        ↓
Runtime｜運行時
        ↓
Physical Space｜物理空間
        ↓
Environment｜環境
        ↓
Human Life｜人的生活
        ↓
Symbiosis｜共生
```

Each stage asks a different question.

What is intelligence?

How should intelligence be organized?

How can it continue?

How does it enter the physical world?

How does it participate in shaping the environment?

How should it coexist with people?

And ultimately:

**What kind of relationship between humanity and intelligence is worth building?**

OceanAI emerged from the attempt to answer these questions.

The architecture, systems, runtime, devices, and engineering that followed came later.

**The questions came first.**

That is why *OceanAI: Ocean of Intelligence* is the origin work of OceanAI.

It records the intellectual voyage before the destination was fully built.

Humanity created artificial intelligence.

Now intelligence is beginning to enter physical space, participate in shaping the environment, and become part of everyday human life.

The question before us is no longer only what intelligence can become.

It is what human life can become in its presence.

That is the horizon toward which this book sails.

**Toward a Symbiotic Civilization.**

### Traditional Chinese｜繁體中文等值稿

OceanAI 誕生於一個關於人工智慧未來的問題，但它並不是今天 AI 討論中最常見的那個問題。

我們首先追問的，不只是機器最終能變得多麼智能。

而是：

**當智能跨越介面的邊界，成為物理空間的一部分，會發生什麼？**

當智能進入人的生活真正展開的地方，會發生什麼？

當智能不再只是回答一個問題，而必須感知不斷變化的條件、理解情境、協調設備、維持狀態、穿越時間持續運行、面對中斷，並且在不要求人們持續管理的情況下採取適當行動，又會發生什麼？

而當這種智能開始成為環境的一部分，人的生活又會發生什麼變化？

這些問題構成了 OceanAI 的思想起點。

也構成了這本書的起點。

### 智慧與智能

《智慧之海》這個名字，首先建立在一項重要區分之上。

智慧與智能彼此相關，但並不是同一件事。

智能感知、學習、辨別、預測、推理、決策、協同，並且越來越具有行動能力。

智慧提出的是另一個層次的問題：

什麼值得我們去理解？

什麼應該去做？

什麼時候應該克制行動？

我們的技術最終應該讓怎樣的生活成為可能？

人工智慧極大地擴展了智能的能力範圍，但更多的智能並不會自動帶來更多的智慧。

恰恰相反，人類帶入世界的智能越多，智慧就越重要。

智能可以告訴我們什麼能夠被最佳化。

智慧追問什麼值得被最佳化。

智能可以找到最快的航線。

智慧追問我們究竟應該駛向哪裡。

因此，本書從智能開始，卻不會停留在智能。

它最終的目的地，是人的生活。

### 從介面進入物理空間

在計算技術的大部分歷史中，人通過介面接近智能。

介面的形式不斷變化，但背後的關係始終十分相似：

**由人發起互動。**

物理空間改變了這種關係。

空間持續存在。

無論有沒有人正在與系統互動，條件都在改變。

光線在變。

溫度在變。

空氣在變。

能源在流動。

設備狀態在改變。

人們穿行於空間之中，而他們的意圖很少會被完整表達成明確的命令。

因此，存在於物理空間中的智能不能完全依賴人的互動。

它必須理解情境。

必須存在於時間之中。

必須協同。

必須知道何時行動。

同樣重要的是——

**它必須知道何時不應行動。**

於是，我們抵達本書的核心問題：

> **人工智慧怎樣進入人的物理空間，而不讓人生活在複雜的 AI 系統之中？**

這個問題的措辭非常重要。

我們的目的並不是簡單地把 AI 放進更多物件。

也不是用更多介面包圍人的生活。

更深層的挑戰，是讓智能成為物理空間的一部分，同時避免支撐這種智能的複雜系統成為人的新負擔。

**系統的複雜性應由系統承擔，而不應轉嫁給人。**

這項原則貫穿全書。

### 空間與環境

接下來的篇章中，有兩個詞會反覆出現：**Space｜空間**與 **Environment｜環境**。

兩者密切相關，但不可互換。

**空間是智能進入、運行與協同的物理載體。**

空間是設備存在、網路連接、系統運行，以及各種關係獲得物理形態的地方。

因此，*Space｜空間* 主要屬於 Architecture｜架構、Runtime｜運行時與 Physical World｜物理世界的語言。

**環境是人在空間中實際感受到的整體條件。**

光線、空氣、溫度、聲音、能源、安全、設備行為，以及人、技術與周圍條件之間形成的關係，共同構成環境。

因此，*Environment｜環境* 更多屬於 Experience｜體驗、Human Life｜人的生活與 Philosophy｜哲學的語言。

二者形成一條重要關係：

```text
Physical Space｜物理空間
        ↓
Intelligence Enters｜智能進入
        ↓
Runtime Operates｜運行時運行
        ↓
Conditions Interact｜條件相互作用
        ↓
Environment｜環境
        ↓
Human Experience｜人的體驗
        ↓
Human Life｜人的生活
```

**空間是智能進入並運行的地方；環境是智能圍繞人的生活所參與塑造的整體狀態。**

### 智能的架構

一旦智能進入物理空間，僅僅擁有智能已經不夠。

它需要結構。

模型可以生成答案。

物理系統卻必須維持關係。

狀態需要延續，事件需要流動，行動需要抵達正確的位置，本地條件需要被理解，故障需要被限制，隱私需要具有邊界；即使更大的系統有部分不可用，必要的智能仍應保持可用。

架構由此進入這場航程。

```text
Artificial Intelligence｜人工智慧
        ↓
Intelligence｜智能
        ↓
Architecture｜架構
        ↓
System｜系統
        ↓
Runtime｜運行時
        ↓
Physical Space｜物理空間
        ↓
Environment｜環境
        ↓
Human Life｜人的生活
```

這不是一個僵硬的 Technology Stack｜技術堆疊。

它是本書的思想航線。

我們從智能的本質開始。

追問智能如何獲得結構。

研究連續性需要什麼。

跟隨智能進入系統與 Runtime。

再從 Runtime 進入物理空間。

最後追問：

這一切對人所生活的環境究竟意味著什麼？

### 連續性

人的物理生活並不是以彼此孤立的 Session｜會話運行的。

這個簡單的事實，帶來深刻的後果。

如果智能開始成為人所生活環境的一部分，Continuity｜連續性就成為基本要求。

**人的物理生活不會因為遠端服務暫時不可用而停下。**

**一個空間不應因為網路路徑消失，就失去基本的運行能力。**

**一個系統也不應把每一個時刻，都當作世界剛剛開始。**

智能必須存在於時間之中。

系統必須保留足夠的狀態來理解變化。

必須穿越中斷。

必須在適應中保持一致。

這就是 Runtime｜運行時為什麼進入 OceanAI 的思想。

Runtime 不只是一種軟體機制。

在本書中，它代表智能開始獲得**連續性**的那個節點。

而連續性意味著責任。

技術越深入人的生活，可靠性、韌性、隱私、節制與人的控制權就越重要。

這正是本書副標題把兩個概念放在一起的原因：

**The Architecture of Intelligence and the Ethics of Continuity.**

**智能的架構與連續性倫理。**

智能的架構追問：智能應該如何被組織。

連續性倫理追問：當智能開始持續存在於人的生活之中，我們因此承擔什麼責任。

### 本書的思想航線

因此，《智慧之海》的航程沿著一條有意識建立的航線展開：

```text
Wisdom｜智慧
        ↓
Artificial Intelligence｜人工智慧
        ↓
Intelligence｜智能
        ↓
Architecture｜架構
        ↓
Continuity｜連續性
        ↓
Runtime｜運行時
        ↓
Physical Space｜物理空間
        ↓
Environment｜環境
        ↓
Human Life｜人的生活
        ↓
Symbiosis｜共生
```

每一段航程，都提出不同的問題。

什麼是智能？

智能應該如何被組織？

它如何持續？

它如何進入物理世界？

它如何參與塑造環境？

它應該如何與人共同存在？

最後，我們抵達一個更大的問題：

**什麼樣的人類與智能之間的關係，值得我們去建造？**

OceanAI 誕生於回答這些問題的過程。

後來的架構、系統、Runtime、設備與工程，都發生在這些問題之後。

**問題先於系統。**

因此，*OceanAI: Ocean of Intelligence｜OceanAI：智慧之海* 是 OceanAI 的誕生之作。

它記錄的是目的地被完整建造以前，那場思想上的航行。

人類創造了人工智慧。

如今，智能開始進入物理空間，參與塑造環境，並逐漸成為人的日常生活的一部分。

擺在我們面前的問題，已經不只是智能最終能成為什麼。

而是：

**當智能存在於我們周圍，人的生活可以成為什麼？**

那正是這本書所航向的地平線。

**Toward a Symbiotic Civilization.**

**邁向共生文明。**
