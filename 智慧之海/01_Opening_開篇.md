# Opening｜開篇

## The Voyage Begins｜航程開始

### English Source｜American English

Humanity lived with wisdom long before it learned how to build intelligence.

We recognized it in memory and judgment, in the instincts of living things, in the rhythms of nature, and in the sailor who learned to read the wind before the storm arrived.

We called some of it knowledge.

Some instinct.

Some experience.

And at its highest human expression, wisdom.

Then humanity began to make intelligence.

At first, it lived inside machines.

Then inside networks.

Then inside models that could see, listen, predict, generate language, and reason with us.

For a long time, we approached intelligence through interfaces.

We learned how to enter the machine's world—how to ask, command, search, and wait for a response.

Now something is changing.

**Intelligence is beginning to enter ours.**

It is crossing the interface and entering physical space—the world in which human life actually unfolds.

And once intelligence enters space, it encounters something no interface can fully contain:

**life in continuity.**

A space does not begin when an interface opens.

Life does not end when an interaction ends.

Sunlight moves.

Air changes.

People arrive and leave.

Energy flows.

Time passes.

Life continues.

Intelligence that enters this world must learn to continue with it.

This is where our voyage begins.

---

Why an ocean?

Because intelligence is ceasing to resemble a single machine.

A machine has a boundary.

An interface has an edge.

An ocean does not.

It has depth and movement, visible currents and hidden ones. It connects distant shores without making them identical. It is continuous, yet never still.

Every wave is local.

No wave exists apart from the sea.

Perhaps intelligence is beginning to acquire something of this character.

Distributed, yet connected.

Local, yet part of something larger.

Continuous, yet always changing.

Moving among machines, networks, spaces, environments, and human life.

This is the ocean we are entering.

Between wisdom and intelligence lies a question of what humanity can create—and what it should create.

Between intelligence and human life lies a question of how technology should belong in the world.

Between where we stand and the horizon lies an ocean.

This book is an attempt to navigate it.

Not to conquer it.

Not to reduce it to an architecture diagram.

But to understand its depths, its currents, its islands, its flocks, and the shores toward which it may carry us.

OceanAI began as a name for this voyage.

And the voyage begins with a question:

> **How can artificial intelligence enter our physical spaces without making us live inside complex AI systems?**

The horizon is before us.

Let us begin.

**OceanAI: Ocean of Intelligence**

*Toward a Symbiotic Civilization.*

### Traditional Chinese｜繁體中文等值稿

在人類學會如何建造智能以前，我們早已與智慧共同生活。

我們在記憶與判斷中認識它，在生命的本能中感受到它，在自然的節律中看見它，也在水手於風暴到來之前閱讀風向的能力中依靠它。

我們把其中一些稱為知識。

一些稱為本能。

一些稱為經驗。

而當它抵達人更高層次的精神表達，我們稱之為智慧。

後來，人類開始製造智能。

最初，它存在於機器之中。

然後進入網路。

再後來，它進入能夠觀看、聆聽、預測、生成語言，甚至與我們共同推理的模型。

很長一段時間，我們通過介面接近智能。

我們學會如何進入機器的世界——如何詢問、命令、搜尋，以及等待回應。

現在，有些事情正在改變。

**智能開始進入我們的世界。**

它正在跨越介面，進入物理空間——進入人的生活真正展開的世界。

而當智能進入空間，它就會遇見一種任何介面都無法完全容納的事物：

**連續發生的生活。**

空間不會因為介面被打開才開始存在。

生活也不會因為一次互動結束就停止。

陽光移動。

空氣變化。

人們到來，又離開。

能源流動。

時間向前。

生活持續。

進入這個世界的智能，必須學會與它一起持續。

我們的航程，由此開始。

---

為什麼是海洋？

因為智能正在失去「一台機器」的形狀。

機器有邊界。

介面有邊緣。

海洋沒有。

它有深度，也有流動；有看得見的洋流，也有隱藏在深處的暗流。它連接遙遠的海岸，卻不要求每一片海岸變得相同。它持續存在，卻從不靜止。

每一道浪都是局部的。

但沒有一道浪能脫離海洋而存在。

也許，智能正在開始呈現某些相似的特徵。

分散存在，卻彼此連接。

立足本地，卻屬於更大的整體。

持續存在，卻始終變化。

它開始在機器、網路、空間、環境與人的生活之間流動。

這就是我們正在進入的海洋。

在智慧與智能之間，存在著「人類能夠創造什麼」與「人類應該創造什麼」的追問。

在智能與人的生活之間，存在著「技術應該如何存在於這個世界」的追問。

而在我們此刻站立的地方與地平線之間，是一片海洋。

這本書，就是一次試圖穿越它的航行。

不是為了征服它。

不是為了把它壓縮成一張架構圖。

而是為了理解它的深度、洋流、島嶼與鳥群，以及它可能把我們帶往的海岸。

**OceanAI 最初，是這場航程的一個名字。**

而這場航程，始於一個問題：

> **人工智慧怎樣進入人的物理空間，而不讓人生活在複雜的 AI 系統之中？**

地平線就在前方。

讓我們啟航。

**OceanAI：智慧之海**

*邁向共生文明。*
